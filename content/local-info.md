## Quran 101

Let The Power Of The Holy Quran Illuminate Your Soul, Enrich Your Mind, and Nourish Your Heart

The Quran is the fundamental book of Islam and God’s final guidance to humanity. It’s the living and breathing divine writ that informs the decisions of Billions every day.

The purpose of these weekly gatherings is to allow our friends from different faith backgrounds to experience this great book firsthand, meet and engage in friendly discussions with Muslims in a courteous environment, and share their experiences.

We aim at building bridges that lead to mutual respect and understanding while providing valuable information to those who would like to learn more and explore the Holy Quran.

A free copy of the English translation of the Holy Quran will be available. Refreshments & light snacks will be provided.
