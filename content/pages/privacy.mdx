We respect your privacy. This site does not show advertising or sell your information.

## What we collect

When you fill out a form (for example, to contact us or request a free Quran), we collect only what you enter: typically your name, email, phone number, mailing address, and message.

## How we use it

We use your information **only** to respond to you, mail what you requested, or connect you with the support you asked for. We never share it with third parties except the service that delivers our form messages.

## Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time through our [contact page](/contact).

## Site statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are most helpful, such as how many people view a page and how long they read it. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives your browser type and approximate location, with your IP address anonymized. We do not use it to identify you. You can opt out with Google's [browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Embedded videos

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play.
