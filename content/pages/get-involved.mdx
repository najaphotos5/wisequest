<h2 id="volunteer">Volunteer</h2>

We are always looking for friendly volunteers to help with Quran 101 sessions, information booths, new-Muslim mentoring, mailing free Qurans, and this website. No experience needed, just good character and a willing heart.

<h2 id="donate">Donate</h2>

Your support pays for free Qurans, books, postage, refreshments at Quran 101, and booth materials. Donations are made through **ICNA Sacramento**.
