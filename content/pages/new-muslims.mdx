Welcome to the family. Whether you embraced Islam today or are about to, you are not alone. Take things one step at a time: Islam is a journey, and every sincere step counts.

## Your roadmap

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect with a mentor">
A friendly, experienced Muslim can answer your questions and support you through the early days. [Ask us to connect you](/contact?reason=new-muslim).
</Step>
<Step title="Learn to pray">
Prayer is your daily connection with God. Start with what you can, and learn the rest gradually. [How to pray](/new-muslims/how-to-pray).
</Step>
<Step title="Read the Quran">
A few verses a day in a translation you understand. [Get a free copy](/resources/free-quran).
</Step>
<Step title="Find your community">
Visit a [local mosque](/local/mosques) or join a Quran 101 session. Most mosques run programs for new Muslims.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep learning, gently">
Use the toolkit below, and don't hesitate to ask. Our [glossary](/glossary) explains common Arabic terms.
</Step>
</Steps>
