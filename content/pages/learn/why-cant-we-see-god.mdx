Dr. Phillips, I understand, brother. I wrestled with similar questions while searching for the truth. God then guided me to Islam, where I found answers that made sense to me. If God revealed Himself beyond all doubt, faith would no longer involve a meaningful choice. I’ll explain why.

1- God guides those who sincerely seek His guidance and genuinely search for the truth. Once you recognize the truth, you must choose whether to accept it; He does not force belief upon you.

2- Seeing God’s Majesty is the greatest reward and honor, reserved for the people of Paradise. Does everyone deserve such an honor in this life?

3- For the sake of argument, suppose God revealed Himself to us in this life. Would human curiosity end there, or would people demand further proof? Some might still deny Him and transgress. Imagine the seriousness of denying God after seeing Him. I therefore view His remaining unseen as an act of mercy.

4- Imagine being hired by a large company but refusing to work until you personally meet its founder. Although God has honored humanity (“Indeed, We have dignified the children of Adam” \[Quran 17:70\]), we must remain humble and recognize our limits as a minute speck compared to the rest of His creation. God does not need our belief; we need it. Recognizing Him and believing in Him benefits us.

5- When you see a masterpiece, you do not need to meet the artist to recognize the talent behind it; the work itself reveals the maker’s skill. Likewise, we can recognize God through His creation. Consider the human body: the intricate work of our organs, nervous system, brain cells, and DNA. If all the DNA in one human body were stretched end to end, it could circle Earth approximately 1.5 million times. And that is only the human body; consider also the oceans, the sun, the moon, the galaxies, and the wider universe.

6- This life is a test. If a teacher revealed the answers during an exam, the test would lose its purpose. Likewise, we are meant to recognize God by using the faculties He has given us.

7- How can we identify the true religion? God is not the author of confusion; His message has remained consistent throughout history. All prophets, including Jesus, taught people to worship God alone, without images, statues, or idols, and to recognize and respect every messenger and prophet He sent. Which religion adheres strictly to this message today? Islam teaches worship of the one true God, the God worshipped by Jesus, Moses, and Abraham, and recognizes all the prophets sent by God.
