<CompareTopic num={3} title="No Original Sin">
<CompareView kind="islam">Humans are born pure; no inherited sin (Quran 35:18)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">‘Let the little children come to me, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.’ (Matthew 19:14)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">The Church teaches original sin from Adam & Eve.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={4} title="Salvation Through Righteous Deeds">
<CompareView kind="islam">Faith + Good Deeds = Salvation (Quran 2:277, 103:1-3)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">‘If you want to enter life, keep the commandments.’ (Matthew 19:17)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">The Church teaches salvation by faith in Jesus’ sacrifice alone.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={6} title="No Intermediary Between God and Man">
<CompareView kind="islam">Direct prayer to God, no clergy needed (Quran 2:186)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">‘Pray to your Father in secret.’ (Matthew 6:6)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">The Church promotes priestly intercession and confession to clergy.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={7} title="Dietary Laws & Worship Practices">
<CompareView kind="islam">Follows halal laws, prays with prostration (Quran 5:3, 22:77)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">Jesus followed kosher laws and prayed in prostration (Matthew 26:39)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">The Church abolished dietary laws and changed prayer postures.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={11} title="Fasting">
<CompareView kind="islam">Fasting (Sawm) is a major act of worship (Quran 2:183)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">Jesus fasted for 40 days (Matthew 4:2)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">Many churches do not emphasize fasting, except for Lent.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={13} title="No Worship of Idols or Images">
<CompareView kind="islam">Islam strictly forbids idolatry (Quran 6:74, 21:52)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">Jesus opposed idol worship (Exodus 20:4-5, Matthew 4:10)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">Many churches have statues, images, and crosses for worship.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={14} title="Circumcision">
<CompareView kind="islam">Required for males in Islam (Quran 16:123)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">Jesus was circumcised on the eighth day (Luke 2:21)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">Most Christian denominations abandoned circumcision.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={15} title="Jesus Had a Mission to the Lost Sheep of Israel">
<CompareView kind="islam">Islam confirms Jesus’ mission was to Israelites (Quran 3:49)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">‘I was sent only to the lost sheep of Israel.’ (Matthew 15:24)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">The Church teaches Jesus’ mission was for the whole world.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={17} title="God Alone Has Supreme Authority">
<CompareView kind="islam">No one shares God’s power (Quran 5:72)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">‘My Father is greater than I.’ (John 14:28)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">Church doctrine teaches Jesus is co-equal with God.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={18} title="No Concept of the “Son of God”">
<CompareView kind="islam">God does not beget children (Quran 19:35)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">Jesus called himself the ‘Son of Man,’ not ‘Son of God.’ (Luke 22:69-70)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">The Church insists Jesus is the literal Son of God.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
<CompareTopic num={19} title="God Cannot Be Seen">
<CompareView kind="islam">No one can see God directly (Quran 6:103)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="jesus">‘No one has ever seen God.’ (John 1:18)</CompareView>
<CompareView kind="church">The Church teaches Jesus is God in human form.</CompareView>
</CompareTopic>
