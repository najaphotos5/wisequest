Becoming Muslim does not require a ceremony, a fee, or a long course of study. It rests on two beliefs held sincerely in your heart:

<Steps>
<Step title="Believe in the One True God">
There is only One God, the Creator of everything and the Giver of every blessing. He alone deserves our gratitude and worship. He does not resemble any of His creation, and He has no partner, son, daughter, or spouse.
</Step>
<Step title="Recognize Muhammad ﷺ as His final messenger">
Muhammad ﷺ is the last in a long chain of prophets and messengers that includes Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, and Jesus (peace be upon them all). [Learn about Prophet Muhammad ﷺ](/learn/muhammad).
</Step>
<Step title="Declare your faith (the Shahada)">
Affirm this belief by saying the testimony of faith, ideally in Arabic and in your own language.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Why it matters

- **It is the first step of being Muslim.** Whoever intends to become Muslim makes this declaration and believes in it.
- **It is the first step toward Paradise.** The Prophet ﷺ said, “Whoever dies knowing that there is no god but Allah will enter Paradise.” (Sahih Muslim 26)
- **It is a promise of salvation.** The Prophet ﷺ said, “Allah has forbidden the Fire for anyone who says ‘There is none worthy of worship except Allah,’ seeking by it the Face of Allah.” (Sahih al-Bukhari 425)
- **Your past is forgiven.** Embracing Islam wipes the slate clean. You begin a fresh, new life. (Sahih Muslim 121)

## What happens next?

Nobody expects you to know everything on day one. Islam is learned step by step, at your own pace.

1. **Your first week:** Learn the basics of prayer and start with what you can. [How to pray](/new-muslims/how-to-pray).
2. **Your first month:** Meet a mentor, visit a [local mosque](/local/mosques), and read a little of the [Quran](/learn/quran) each day.
3. **Keep growing:** Join a new-Muslim circle and explore our [new Muslim toolkit](/new-muslims).
