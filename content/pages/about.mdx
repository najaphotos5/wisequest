**WiseQuest** is an independent, volunteer-run project based in the Greater Sacramento area of California. We are ordinary Muslims (neighbors, parents, students, and professionals) who want to share the beautiful, timeless message of all prophets with love, care, and respect.

## What we do

- **Answer questions** about Islam, sincerely and without pressure, by email, phone, or in person.
- **Host Quran 101**, a relaxed weekly gathering where people of all faiths can explore the Quran and talk with Muslims.
- **Give away free Qurans** and books to anyone who asks.
- **Support new Muslims** with mentors, guides, and a welcoming community.

## Our approach

We believe faith is a personal choice: *“There is no compulsion in religion”* (Quran 2:256). Our role is simply to share information honestly and answer questions as best we can. Whatever you decide, you are always welcome.

## Our content

The articles on this site draw on the Quran, authentic hadith collections, and the work of respected scholars and authors. Quotations from the Quran are translations of its meaning; the original is in Arabic. If you spot a mistake, please [let us know](/contact).

## Affiliation

This is an independent site run by local volunteers. It is **not officially affiliated with WhyIslam**. Donations made through this site support local outreach through [ICNA Sacramento](https://www.icnasac.org/donate).
