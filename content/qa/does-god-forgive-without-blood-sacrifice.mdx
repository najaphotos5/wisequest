---
title: Does God forgive sins without a blood sacrifice?
description: >-
  Yes. The Quran describes a God who forgives all sins for those who sincerely
  repent.
category: Sin & forgiveness

---
Yes, indeed. In Islam, no sacrifice, intermediary, or payment is needed for forgiveness. God is *Al-Ghafur*, the Most Forgiving, and *Ar-Rahim*, the Most Merciful. Every person can turn to Him directly, at any time, and He promises to forgive those who sincerely repent: who feel genuine regret, stop the wrong, and resolve not to return to it.

<Verse cite="Quran 4:27">
God intends to redeem you, but those who follow their desires want you to turn away utterly.
</Verse>

<Verse cite="Quran 4:147">
What would God accomplish by your punishment, if you have given thanks, and have believed? God is Appreciative and Cognizant.
</Verse>

<Verse cite="Quran 39:53">
Say, [O Prophet, that Allah says,] “O My servants who have exceeded the limits against their souls! Do not lose hope in Allah’s mercy, for Allah certainly forgives all sins. He is indeed the All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.”
</Verse>

Islam also teaches that every soul is responsible only for its own deeds, so there is no inherited sin to be paid for. See [Is this life a punishment for the sin of Adam?](/qa/is-life-a-punishment-for-original-sin)
